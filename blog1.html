5 Keto Mistakes People Over 50 Make

The ketogenic diet can be powerful — especially for those over 50 — but only when done right. Here are 5 mistakes to avoid:

  • Not eating enough fat: Keto is a high-fat diet. Skipping fats leads to low energy.
  • Cutting carbs too fast: Gradual carb reduction helps reduce "keto flu" symptoms.
  • Ignoring electrolytes: Sodium, potassium, and magnesium matter more than ever.
  • Not tracking food: Use apps to understand your macros and adjust them.
  • Impatience: Keto isn’t a magic switch — give it 2–4 weeks to really see results.

Consistency and awareness of your body's needs make all the difference. Stay hydrated, stay fueled, and stay patient.
